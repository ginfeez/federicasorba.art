Developed through [Nova Media Studio](https://novamediastudio.co)
Artico Club is an audiovisual project developed for the winter programme of Artico Festival, transforming the Teatro Politeama Boglione in Bra into a temporary club. The live set consisted in creating a video mapping projection on the wall, inside a decorative frame frieze on the wall.

![](assets/img/artico/artico_1.webp)

{right} For the live performance of Fine Before You Came, the visual design was conceived specifically for the theatre and for the atmosphere of the band’s latest album, C’è ancora amore. Rather than treating the visuals as a backdrop to the concert, the project explores how light, image, architecture and sound can coexist within the same spatial language. The motion graphics created, followed the brand identity of the festival created by Ortica Studio (MI).

![](assets/img/artico/artico_12.mp4)
![](assets/img/artico/artico_13.webp)

{left large} The work extends beyond the stage. Visual interventions move through the theatre, revealing its architecture in unexpected ways and connecting the stage, foyer and surrounding spaces. The audience becomes part of an environment where music and images continuously influence the perception of the space.

![](assets/img/artico/artico_2.jpg)
![](assets/img/artico/artico_3.mp4)

{left large} The year before, the collaboration expanded towards the DJs of Respiri Festival, in Melle. The visual system became more audio-reactive, allowing graphics and movement to respond directly to the rhythm and dynamics of the music.

The visual language followed the theme of botanica: organic shapes, vegetal structures and growing forms became a visual vocabulary that could continuously transform in response to sound. Instead of illustrating the theme literally, botanical elements became a system — something alive, responsive and constantly evolving with the music.

![](assets/img/artico/artico_4.webp)
![](assets/img/artico/artico_5.mp4)

{left large} In 2023, the project took another direction through a collaboration with Alessandro Cortini and Fonorama.

Here, the visual approach became more abstract and elemental. Form, light, texture and movement were stripped back from recognizable imagery, creating a visual environment closer to an audiovisual composition.
