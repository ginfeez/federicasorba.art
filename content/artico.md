Artico Club tooks place at Teatro Politeama Boglione, one of the most famous theatre in Bra (CN). The live set consisted in creating a video mapping projection on the wall, inside a decorative frame frieze on the wall.

In 2023 the Dj set and live visuals has been performed on the moment before and after Alessandro Cortini concert.

![](assets/img/artico/artico_1.webp)

{right} Drawing on the theme of the beyond and the concept of passage, Beyond the Portal reimagines the Duomo Hall as a liminal space — a threshold between realms of perception. Through site-specific projection mapping, the architecture was transformed into a dynamic, virtual non-portal: an ephemeral yet powerful gateway that challenges the boundaries between physical structure and digital expression.

![](assets/img/artico/artico_7.webp)
![](assets/img/artico/artico_3.webp)

{left large} The experience unfolds through a continuous interplay of light, sound, movement, and architecture. Carefully choreographed visual sequences respond to the geometry of the space, shifting its scale and perception while guiding the audience through an evolving sensory journey. Rather than simply occupying the architecture, the installation activates it, creating moments of tension, suspension, and transformation where the physical and the virtual become increasingly indistinguishable.

![](assets/img/artico/artico_12.mp4)
![](assets/img/artico/artico_13.webp)


{left large} Beyond the Portal ultimately becomes an exploration of transition — a passage into a space that exists between the tangible and the imagined. As light and imagery reshape the monumental structure, the familiar environment is suspended and reconfigured, inviting the audience to question what lies beyond the visible. The installation is conceived not as a portal to another place, but as a portal to another state of perception.
