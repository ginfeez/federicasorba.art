{left indent large} The New Gastronome is an online magazine brought by The University of Gastronomic Sciences of Pollenzo, Italy and is the result of our creative engine, a rotating team of professionals that include alumni,  students and professors, coming from all over the world, who shine a light on the individuality and uniqueness of food.

{right} Connect with people and different cultures throughout our global gastronomic community with impactful articles, stories and features, photo essays, videos, how-tos, recipes, and much more.​​​​​​​ 

![](assets/img/baby_gastronome/baby_gastronome_2.webp)

{left large} In this particular issue, we reached out to some gastro-parents, asking to share their experiences and thoughts about raising a child, not gabbing about standard parent talk but instead focusing on how they make their kids aware of the food they eat. So get ready because you will find freaky articles with unbelievable information or anthropological aspects with an international view. There are many different sections inside the issue, such as stories and myths linked to the topic of maternity and births, but even also tips and fun facts, recepies and so on.
