Federica Sorba is a Graphic Designer and New Media Artist. With a background in the arts, she has been engaging with the world of new media for years, working primarily in real-time visuals and live performances. Her work has led her to collaborate with various artists, museums, and Italian cultural organizations such as Italian Institute of Culture in Nairobi, Iosonouncane, Daniela Pes, Gio Evan, Club2Club, Recontemporary, OGR, Seminaria Sogni in Terra, Club Silencio, and Artico Festival. In 2024 she founded [Nova Media Studio](https://novamediastudio.co) together with media and sound artist Eduard Tampu with which they aspire to bring unique audio-visual experiences into multiple context contexts.
