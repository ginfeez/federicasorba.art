{left indent large} *Brewing A Better World* is a guide for who wants to explore and dive the deep world of coffee with a focus on specialty coffee. The booklet wants to be an informative content but also a leaflet full of artworks and visual contents. We wanted to create a link between the botanical/informative content about coffee, highlighting the theme of Good, Clean and Fair Coffee made by Slow Food Coffee Coalition, and the world of Specialty Coffee.

![](assets/img/brewing/brewing_12.jpg)

{right small} There are many topics that have been developed, like chapters about the environment and the growing of the coffee seeds, and the journey of itself from planting to our daily cup. Some chapters go deep into the aim of roasters and the different phases, which is linked to the secret of the processing techniques.

![](assets/img/brewing/brewing_3.jpg)
![](assets/img/brewing/brewing_4.jpg)


{left large} The booklet also highlights the importance of sustainability, ethical production, and the value of every person involved throughout the coffee supply chain. By combining scientific information, storytelling, and visual narratives, it offers readers a broader understanding of coffee as both an agricultural product and a cultural experience, encouraging a more conscious and informed appreciation of every cup.
