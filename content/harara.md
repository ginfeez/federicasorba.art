*Harara* means **Heat** in arabic, and that’s the name of this project *Harara* explores **Bastiaan Woudt**'s collections of images taken in Morocco. Images in which the color black prevails, a warm black.

Hence the concept of warmth, of belonging on which the whole zine is based. Divided into six chapters, where each of them is opened by an interview and a Moroccan girl who preludes the content of the chapter, Harara aims to get closer to the user thanks to the real testimonies of a Moroccan girl. The concept of warmth and belonging is explicit through the cover made with heat-sensitive ink that offers a law, to leave one’s mark.
