{left indent large} This series collects a selection of hand-inked illustrations inspired by the world of gastronomy and raw ingredients. Each subject is carefully observed and drawn to highlight its distinctive forms, textures, and natural details, ranging from vegetables, fruits, and spices to fish, flowers, and everyday culinary objects.

![](assets/img/inked_gastronomy/inked_gastronomy_1.webp)

{right} Originally created as individual ink studies, the illustrations are designed to work both as a cohesive visual archive and as standalone pieces. Their clear linework and balanced compositions also make them suitable as tattoo designs, translating botanical and gastronomic subjects into timeless, minimal illustrations.

![](assets/img/inked_gastronomy/inked_gastronomy_2.webp)
![](assets/img/inked_gastronomy/inked_gastronomy_3.webp)

{left large} By combining scientific observation with a graphic approach, the collection celebrates the beauty of ordinary ingredients and the relationship between food, nature, and craftsmanship. The project explores how illustration can transform familiar objects into visual narratives, preserving their character through the expressive qualities of ink.

![](assets/img/inked_gastronomy/inked_gastronomy_4.webp)

{left large} In the years, I made some works for different clients related to the University of Gastronomic Sciences in Pollenzo, like Slow Food Coffee Coalition for which I designed a Coffee Plant, core image of their identity in 2024. I also realized some items related to raw and pure materials linked to the world of gastronomy, that become the cover of the notebook of University of Gastronomic Sciences in 2022.
