{left indent large} The visual concept draws from the language of the opening sequence of the homonymous TV series, particularly its photographic treatment and the interplay between two intersecting axes of imagery. This approach was reinterpreted through generative art, with visuals created in TouchDesigner and dynamically responding to the audio in real time.

![](assets/img/schwarze_materie/schwarze_materie_1.webp)
![](assets/img/schwarze_materie/schwarze_materie_2.webp)

{right} The visual experience was developed specifically for an auditorium setting, where the content was presented across a wide-format screen. A neon light element positioned in the background framed the performance, creating a spatial dialogue between the projected visuals, the architecture, and the performer.​ 

![](assets/img/schwarze_materie/schwarze_materie_3.webp)
![](assets/img/schwarze_materie/schwarze_materie_4.webp)

{left large} The colour temperature of both the visuals and the lighting follows a gradual, ascending progression, evolving throughout the piece and intensifying towards its final section. This controlled shift in colour and light reinforces the work’s overall sense of tension and culminates in a heightened visual climax.
