{left indent large} Design of the brand identity for the Pollenzo 20th Anniversary was an opportunity to translate twenty years of history, research, and gastronomic culture from the University of Gastronomic Sciences into a visual language. The goal was to create an identity that celebrated this important milestone while going beyond a simple anniversary, expressing the spirit of a vibrant, international, and constantly evolving community.

![](assets/img/twenty_years/twenty_years_1.webp)

{right} I developed a flexible visual system designed to support every touchpoint of the event, from institutional communication and environmental graphics to editorial materials and digital content. The brand identity was conceived to highlight the dialogue between tradition and innovation, reflecting both the university's values and the unique atmosphere of the village of Pollenzo.​​​​​​​ 

![](assets/img/twenty_years/twenty_years_2.webp)
![](assets/img/twenty_years/twenty_years_3.webp)

{left large} From May 29 to June 3, 2024, the visual identity accompanied six days of markets, conferences, tastings, workshops, and cultural events, helping to create a cohesive and recognizable experience for students, faculty, alumni, producers, and food industry experts. Seeing the identity come to life through the Alumni Market, discussions on the future of food, and activities organized in collaboration with the Bank of Wine was the moment when design became an integral part of the event experience.
