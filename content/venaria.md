Developed through [Nova Media Studio](https://novamediastudio.co)
Dreaming is a site-specitic visual installation created tor the
Sala di Diana at the Reggia di Venaria, a UNESCO World Heritage site, developed in dialogue with a live string concert.
The project transformed the historic space through advanced video mapping techniques, using light and motion to reinterpret the architectural and sculptural richness of the venue.

![](assets/img/venaria/novamedia-venaria_main.mp4)

{right} The visual design was conceived to seamlessly integrate with the existing stuccoes, reliefs and statues, enhancing their volumes rather than obscuring them. Abstract, fluid, and symmetrical compositions were mapped onto the complex surfaces of the architcure, allowing the visuals to follow and amplify the rhythm and geometry of the space.

{left large} The slow and introspective rhythm of the narration reflects the cyclic nature of daily gestures, their simplicity, and guides both sight and hearing toward the details that can only be perceived in contexts like this one.

![](assets/img/venaria/novamedia_venaria_3.jpg)
![](assets/img/venaria/novamedia_venaria_4.jpg)


{left large} The work invites us to experience this (re)connection as a passage: from the inside to the outside, from the embrace of the walls to the fabric of the landscape, highlighting the harmonic elements of the territory and its homogeneity, in contrast to the visual and acoustic disorder of the outside world.​
